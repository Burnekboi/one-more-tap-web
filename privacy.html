Privacy Policy for One More Tap

Effective Date: September 19, 2026 • Last Updated: September 19, 2026 • Contact: niconan.shaun1128@gmail.com

Quick Summary: One More Tap is an ultra-fast reflex game. We do not collect personal identity information (names, passwords, phone numbers, or emails) on any backend servers. All your game progress, high scores, and audio preferences are stored purely locally on your device or in your TikTok Mini Game cache.

1. Introduction

Welcome to One More Tap ("the Game", "we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains our commitment to player privacy when you access or play One More Tap via the TikTok Mini Game runtime, ByteDance MicroApp platform, or web browsers.

By launching or playing One More Tap, you consent to the handling of information described in this Policy.

2. Information Collection & Storage

A. Local Device Storage (No Server Database)

One More Tap operates client-side. The Game stores gameplay metrics locally on your device using client-side storage APIs (tt.setStorageSync / localStorage), including:

  • High scores for Weird Mode (24 stages) and Crazy Mode (50 stages)
  • Lifetime runs and cumulative tap count
  • Highest consecutive combo streaks
  • Achievement unlock status
  • Daily challenge participation and daily best score
  • Audio and haptic settings (sound on / muted)

This data remains stored on your local device so that your progress is remembered between play sessions. It is never uploaded to any proprietary developer database.

B. TikTok Mini Game Platform Telemetry

When running inside TikTok, TikTok / ByteDance may collect standard technical and device information (device model, operating system, network type, anonymous user ID) to facilitate game execution and social features. Such processing is governed by the TikTok Privacy Policy.

C. Rewarded Video Ads

One More Tap features optional Rewarded Video Advertisements (via tt.createRewardedVideoAd). Watching a short advertisement allows players to revive their run at the current stage with a replenished 10-second timer bank. Ad serving, verification, and measurement are managed directly by the TikTok Mini Games Ad Network. We do not receive, store, or sell any personal ad targeting data.

3. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA & GDPR-K)

One More Tap is a general-audience casual game suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the EEA). If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please notify us at niconan.shaun1128@gmail.com and we will immediately take steps to remove it.

4. Data Deletion & Player Rights

Because your game data is stored purely locally on your mobile device, you retain full control over it at all times. You can delete all your stored scores, achievements, and settings by:

  • Clearing the TikTok application cache in your mobile device settings, or
  • Removing the Mini Game from your TikTok recent games list, or
  • Clearing your browser local storage / website data.

5. Security

We prioritize player security by maintaining a minimal-data architecture. Because we do not transmit or store personal credentials on external servers, the risk of data compromise is minimized.
