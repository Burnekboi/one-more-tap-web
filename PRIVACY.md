## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Information Stored Locally on Your Device
One More Tap is designed as a client-side hyper-casual game. We **do not operate external user account databases** or collect personally identifiable information (such as your legal name, physical address, phone number, or email address) through gameplay.

The Game stores the following gameplay metrics locally on your device via standard client-side storage or the TikTok Mini Game Storage API (`tt.setStorageSync` / `localStorage`):
- High scores for Weird Mode and Crazy Mode
- Cumulative run counts and total taps
- Highest combo streaks
- Unlocked achievements
- Daily challenge participation date and daily best score
- Audio preferences (Mute / Sound Enabled)

This data is stored purely on your client device so that your progress is preserved between gameplay sessions.

### B. TikTok Mini Game Platform & Analytics
When you play One More Tap inside the TikTok application:
- TikTok / ByteDance may automatically collect certain device and technical information (such as device model, operating system version, TikTok user identifier, network connection, and region) in accordance with the [TikTok Privacy Policy](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy).
- One More Tap does not receive or store your private TikTok account credentials.

### C. Advertisements & Rewarded Video Ads
One More Tap integrates TikTok Mini Rewarded Video Ads (`tt.createRewardedVideoAd`) to offer optional in-game revives when a player fails a stage.
- When you request an in-game revive by watching a rewarded video ad, the ad request and delivery are handled directly by the TikTok Mini Games Ad Network / ByteDance Advertising SDK.
- The advertising network may collect anonymized advertising identifiers, device metrics, and interaction data to serve contextual or personalized advertisements and prevent ad fraud.
- We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

## 3. How We Use Information

Any information processed by One More Tap is used solely for the following purposes:
1. **Game Functionality:** Maintaining your stage progression, high scores, achievements, and custom preferences.
2. **Rewarded Ad Delivery:** Granting an in-game revive (+10s timer bank) upon confirmed completion of a TikTok Mini Rewarded Video Ad.
3. **Performance Optimization:** Ensuring smooth 60 FPS animation and responsive touch input across various mobile device screens.
